Menu Card

Starters

Sausage Canapes

An assortment of canapés featuring cherry tomatoes and sausage typically includes combinations like a cherry tomato, sausage, and cheese skewer, or a sausage and tomato topping on a slice of bread or a puff pastry base.

Buffalo Wings

Buffalo wings are a popular American appetizer made from chicken wings that are deep-fried and coated in a vinegar-based cayenne pepper hot sauce and melted butter mixture [1]

Fresh Bruschetta

Fresh bruschetta is an Italian appetizer consisting of grilled bread rubbed with garlic and topped with fresh tomatoes, basil, olive oil, and salt.

Guacamole

Guacamole is a traditional Mexican dip made from mashed avocados, lime juice, onions, tomatoes, and various seasonings.

Loaded Nachos

Loaded nachos are a popular Tex-Mex dish consisting of tortilla chips topped with melted cheese, salsa, and sometimes additional ingredients like beans, ground beef, or chicken.

Paneer Tikka

Paneer Tikka is a popular Indian appetizer made by marinating cubes of paneer (Indian cottage cheese) and vegetables in a spiced yogurt mixture, then grilling or baking them until cooked and slightly charred.

Main Course Dish

Biryani

Biryani is a flavorful and aromatic South Asian rice dish made with basmati rice, meat (such as chicken, mutton, or beef), and a blend of spices, often garnished with fried onions, boiled eggs, and fresh herbs.

Butter Chicken

Butter chicken is a popular Indian main course dish made from chicken cooked in a spiced tomato, butter, and cream sauce.

Hamburgers

Hamburgers are a popular American main course dish consisting of a ground beef patty served in a bun, often with lettuce, tomato, cheese, and various condiments.

Paella

Paella is a traditional Spanish rice dish cooked with saffron, various meats, seafood, and vegetables.

Pizza

Pizza is a popular Italian main course dish consisting of a round, flat bread base topped with tomato sauce, cheese, and various toppings such as vegetables, meats, and herbs.

Ramen

Ramen is a Japanese noodle soup dish consisting of Chinese-style wheat noodles served in a meat or fish-based broth, often flavored with soy sauce or miso, and topped with ingredients such as sliced pork, nori (seaweed), menma (bamboo shoots), and green onions.

Desserts

Black Forest Cake

Black Forest Cake is a rich, chocolate sponge cake layered with whipped cream and cherries, originating from Germany.

Cannoli

Cannoli are Italian pastries consisting of tube-shaped shells of fried pastry dough, filled with a sweet, creamy filling containing ricotta cheese.

Chocolate Macarons

Chocolate Macarons are delicate French pastries made with almond flour, egg whites, and cocoa powder, filled with a rich chocolate ganache.

Easy Baklava

Baklava is a rich, sweet pastry made of layers of filo filled with chopped nuts and sweetened with syrup or honey.

Gulab Jamun

Gulab Jamun is a popular Indian dessert made of deep-fried dough balls soaked in a sweet syrup flavored with cardamom and rose water.

Pavlova

Pavlova is a meringue-based dessert named after the Russian ballerina Anna Pavlova, featuring a crisp crust and soft, light inside, typically topped with fruit and whipped cream.
